Privacy

Yorktown Tools is a veteran-owned supplier in Yorktown, Virginia. This page says what we collect when you request a quote or chat with George, and what we do with it. We do not sell your information.

What we collect

If you use a quote form or George, you may give us your name, company, phone, email, job-site address, needed-by date, quantities, and the tools you want. George also sees the messages you type in the chat and which page of this site you opened him from.

Do not put payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or routing numbers in the chat or in a form. We do not take payment there. Card, ACH, and other payment methods are handled separately, after you and Zachary agree on the order.

How we use it

We use it to answer you, price the tools, and deliver to the job. A quote email goes to you and to sales@yorktowntools.com. If that branded email is unavailable, the same request is sent through our existing quote form so the lead is not lost.

Prices George gives are estimates. He may check a short price list we keep, and he may look up a current public shelf price so the estimate is not a guess. He will say when a number needs to be confirmed. An estimate is not a promise that the item is in stock.

Who handles it

This website is a plain set of pages hosted on GitHub Pages. George’s chat is handled by a Cloudflare Worker. The Worker sends the conversation to xAI (Grok) to draft a reply. xAI may use web search to look up a public price. The Worker can send the quote email through Resend. The older quote forms on the site post to Formspree, which we keep as a backup.

Those companies process the information only so we can answer and follow up. We do not run ads from your quote, and we do not build a sold mailing list from it.

How long we keep it

The chat itself is not stored in a database. The quote you submit is kept in the sales inbox, the same way a phone call or an email quote is kept, so Zachary can fill the order and follow up. You can ask us to delete a quote by calling or emailing.
